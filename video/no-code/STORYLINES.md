# Storylines

Picked: **A**.

## A. Before the first meeting (picked)

A moment in someone's day, one continuous take. It opens at 9:50 on a Monday: the Orion go/no-go review is at 10, and its context sits in email, chats and files. The camera travels across one stage holding the real Code screenshots: the prompt, the build, the checks, the preview, the connections, Publish, and a colleague's consent. It pulls back to show the whole journey and ends on "One prompt. A governed app your whole team can open."

## B. Result first

The published briefing app plays first. Then the camera pulls back and rewinds through consent, Publish, the preview, the build and the prompt, before returning to the live app.

## C. The prompt's journey

The prompt text itself is the carrier. It leaves the Create box and dives, in an endless zoom, into each stage. Every screenshot appears inside a detail of the previous one.
